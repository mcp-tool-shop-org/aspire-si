Step 3 is rule 3 of the [next-step plan](2026-10-08-next-step-plan.md), committed in #35 before
step 2 launched. It runs **only if step 2 passes rule 1**: the three 128-prompt control critics
span at most 0.09 in pairwise accuracy. If rule 1 fails, nothing launches, and the critic setup
comes first.

## What it compares

|---|---|---|

## Readout (rule 3, as committed in #35)

The measure is each critic's pairwise accuracy on the 127 judge pairs (`judge_eval.py`, ties
counting half), from step 2's `results-e/judge.json` and step 3's `results-f/judge.json`.

| Result | Reading |
|---|---|
| Mean difference (fine-tune + ASPIRE minus control) of at least 0.10, with all three seeds of one condition above all three of the other | A fine-tune effect |
| Mean difference of at least 0.10, with overlapping seeds | A tendency, reported as such |
| Mean difference under 0.10 | No reliable difference |

Also reported, with no rule attached:
- each critic's prompt-clustered 95% interval;
- the drift exports: fine-tune drift from base, and ASPIRE drift from the fine-tune, compared with
  run 1's directions (cosine);
- next to the critics:
  - **Kev-4B**, the pinned reference judge, read order-averaged: 0.976 on these pairs;
  - the R&D session's **Kev-4B judge fine-tune**, a second judge only, never the reference.

## How it runs

`pod/plan_f.sh 42 43 44` on one `job` pod:
1. **Setup.** The host check and speed probe, then downloads, as in step 2.
2. **Merge.** Each seed's adapter is merged into a bf16 student.
3. **Configs.** The sft-local configs are written.
4. **ASPIRE.** The three runs train side by side, with the progress line every 10 minutes.
5. **Probes and judge.**

**Stop rules, as for step 2:**
- A host that fails the check or the probe is refused.
- At 30 minutes of ASPIRE, the finish is projected:
  - remaining dialogues × the measured minutes per dialogue;
  - plus the training time step 2 measured;
  - plus 30 minutes for probes, judge and pull.

  If that lands past the deadline, the pod stops at once.
- Results are pulled and the pod is shut down before the deadline.

**Price:** set from step 2's measured times before launch, and sent to the Publisher for the spend
go. Step 2 was priced at 8.5 hours (worst case $18.62). Step 3 adds the merge (a few minutes) and
starts from students that may write longer answers.
