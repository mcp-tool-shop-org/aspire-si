# Run report: step 2, critic variance at 128 training prompts (2026-10-08)

**Plan:** [2026-10-08-next-step-plan.md](2026-10-08-next-step-plan.md), step 2, with the rules
committed in #35 before it launched. Run from main at c5cf3e8 (`pod/plan_e.sh 42 43 44`).

## Result

**Rule 1 fails: more prompts do not narrow the spread between seeds.** Across the three 128-prompt
seeds, the critics span 0.181 in pairwise accuracy, exactly as at 32 prompts. Under the plan,
nothing is compared until the critic setup settles, so step 3 (the fine-tune comparison,
[plan](2026-10-08-step-3-plan.md)) does not run.

**Rule 2: more prompts make the critic better.** Every seed rose by 0.10 to 0.13, and the mean went
from 0.635 to 0.745.

## Reading

- **The seeds keep their order.** At both prompt counts, seed 42 is highest, then 44, then 43, and
  each rose by about the same amount. The spread follows the seed, not the prompt set.
  - What the seed sets is unmeasured: the critic head's initialisation, the student's sampling and
    the order of the data are the candidates.
  - That is a hypothesis for the next plan, not a reading.
- **Training more on the same setup moves every seed up together.** The level gain is real, but it
  does not make one run representative of the condition.
- **Any comparison between conditions needs more seeds per condition, or a critic setup with less
  spread.** At a spread of 0.18, three seeds cannot show a difference of 0.10 without overlap.
