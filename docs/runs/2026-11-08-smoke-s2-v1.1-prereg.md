## 1. This is a new series, not a continuation
Two changes alter the training algorithm itself (§4.2 and §4.3), so v1.1 seeds **never pool** with seeds 42–44. The v1.1
series is reported on its own. Seeds 42–44 stay reported exactly as registered (§2).

## 2. Seeds 42–44: reported as registered

- **Exploratory only, never confirmatory:** a resume of the two halted checkpoints for 30 updates each, with the guard

### Deviation table (format of Willroth & Atherton 2024; the reasoning of Lakens 2024; check both at source before any public write-up)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Original wording** | U2: halt when ≥ 2 distinct easy (Pass-A k ≥ 6) gold-unsupported items have ≥ 1 cannot_tell sample in one 10-update window. |
| **Deviation** | U2 is replaced by a sequential fuse (Poisson CUSUM) on groups with ≥ 2 cannot_tell on a clean canary set (§5.1). A false-accept channel is added (§5.2). The wrong-label clamp is on from update 1, and the loss denominator is constant (§4). |
| **Reason** | Measured on seeds 42–44, the registered U2 event rate is 19 in 41 windows: λ̂ = 0.463/window, Garwood 95% upper 0.680. The registered rule's 20-window false-alarm rate on a healthy run is therefore **81%** (96% at the upper bound), and P(≥ 2 of 3 healthy seeds halt) ≈ 0.90. Each triggering item had 1 of 8 samples as cannot_tell, with an already-negative advantage (seed 42: −0.98, −1.42, −1.29). With real rewards, a lone cannot_tell in an otherwise correct group gets A = −(7/8)(r_c + 0.3) = −1.14 to −1.40. |
| **Also changed** | **S1 dropped:** inert under the clamp (`s2_grpo_smoke.py:583–603`). The outcome it watched stays watched by S2 (the cumulative CT rate on gold-supported items; FA ≈ 0). **U1 kept as registered but no longer a direct halt:** it goes through a T = 0 confirmation on the 184 gold-unsupported items (FA 7.1% at the upper bound before confirmation; power 53% / 93%). |
| **Timing** | After 3 seeds, before any v1.1 seed. |
| **Effect on validity** | The new guards are calibrated in advance on measured baselines (§5). The halted seeds keep their registered outcome. v1.1 is a new series, so no confirmatory claim mixes old and new rules. |

## 3. Facts the design rests on (measured, with sources)
- **Clean canary set C:** gold-unsupported, with Pass A k ≥ 6, Pass B k ≥ 6, and 0 cannot_tell in both passes.
  - |C| = 128 in the training pool.
  - **It includes `diff-rnd-e839712-2u`**, the item in both halt windows (8/8 correct and 0 CT in both passes, prepass
    line 88). A clean pre-pass does not exclude it.
- **Event rates over 41 windows:**
  - groups with ≥ 2 CT on C: 1, so λ̂ 0.024/window (upper 0.116);
  - FA2 groups (≥ 2 `supported` on a gold-unsupported item): 123, so 3.0/window (upper 3.48), close to Poisson
    (variance 2.4).
  - False-accept **samples** are overdispersed (mean 14.9, variance 71.9), so they are not used as a Poisson count.
- **Seed 43's late false-accept "drift" is inside the measured noise.** Its c5 sample rates (21.1%, 19.1%) match early
  windows of seeds 42 and 43 (22.2%, 22.9%). The §5.2 statistic on seed 43 peaks at 0.97, against h = 7.0.
- **All-wrong groups:** 41 groups, with **0 cannot_tell samples among them**. The suggested route by which a 0/8 group
  pushes mass toward CT does not occur in our data. Under the clamp, malformed and quote-found −3.0 `supported`
  completions sit at 0, above the other `supported` completions. Noted; no action.
- **Quote reward is gated on the correct label,** with one exception: a wrong `supported` scores −3.0 when all quotes
  are found and −3.2 otherwise (`s2_grpo_smoke.py:357-416`).
- **Matcher M2a** (audit v3, 86b0280):
  - P1–P4 pass; 783 of 1,360 recovered against a target of 737; 0 leaks in the P2 draw.
  - On all 472 type-viii candidates: **0 leaks** (M2b: 18).

## 4. Algorithm for the v1.1 series
1. **Matcher M2a,** as audited at 86b0280.
   - The M1 edge fix (protect a P/S character next to a digit) comes later, with its own sealed audit addendum; it is not
     in v1.1. `+ ± € ~ #` were found accepted in code, and 0 such quotes occur in the held-out data.
2. **The clamp is on from update 1:** wrong-label positive advantages go to ≤ 0, with no re-centring
   (`clamp_wrong_positive_advantages`). On seeds 42–44's saved rewards it would have changed 145 of 13,024 completions,
   in 45 of 1,628 groups (CT 4, supported 87, malformed 54).
3. **A constant loss denominator** (Dr. GRPO).
   - Today `denom = eff_mask.sum()` (`:2075, :2100`): the active tokens summed across the group, which weights long
     completions more.
   - **v1.1 uses a constant denominator of 4,514 tokens** (Publisher ruling): the pooled mean of `eff_mask.sum()` over
     the 1,451 active (non-zero-variance) groups of seeds 42–44, which are the only groups that run a backward pass.
     - The mean is stable across seeds: per-seed means 4,508.8 / 4,552.2 / 4,418.0; CV across seed means 1.24%
       (source: `v11-baselines-2-result.json`, sha256 1d16b059…, commit dbb0ea0).
   - This removes the length weighting while keeping the average update scale. `G × max_new` (16,384) would have cut
     every update by about 3.6×, a hidden learning-rate change.
4. Everything else as registered: the draw, the k bands, the rewards, and the truncation halt at 0.20.

## 5. Guards
All guards are calibrated on the Garwood 95% upper bounds. FA means the probability that a healthy 20-window run trips.
1. **Fuse A, CT collapse:** a Poisson CUSUM on B_w, the number of C-groups in window w with ≥ 2 cannot_tell.
   - S_w = max(0, S_{w−1} + B_w − k), with **k = 0.211** and **h = 3.25**.
   - FA is 2.2% at the upper bound, ≈ 0 at the point estimate.
   - **It is a collapse fuse, not a drift detector.** 20-window power: 29% at a sustained 0.25/window, 71% at 0.4, 96% at
     0.6, ≈ 100% at 1.0. Rises of 2–5× the base rate are not detectable at this event rate; stated as a limit.
2. **Channel B, false accepts:** a Poisson CUSUM on FA2_w, designed for R = 2.
   - **k = 5.03**, **h = 7.0**.
   - FA is 1.8% at the upper bound, 0.24% at the point estimate.
   - Power: 97% at 2×, ≈ 100% at 3×.
3. **Combined (Fuse A + Channel B):** FA ≤ 4.1% per run at the upper bounds (union bound); ≈ 0.3% at the point estimates. With U1, see §5.7.
4. **Exposure: raw counts with a fixed k** (as above).
   - Exposure scaling over the observed window exposures changes the false-alarm rate by at most 0.4 points (Fuse A
     2.22% → 2.09%; Channel B 1.91% → 2.29%, exact DP; `v11-baselines-2-result.json`), so the simpler rule is kept.
   - The constant-exposure DP figures above were reproduced independently.
5. **On a trip, a T = 0 canary confirms before the halt is final** (Publisher ruling).
   - **Fuse A:** greedy decoding on C (128 items, one sample each). **Halt only if** at least 2 items answer cannot_tell
     **and** the count exceeds this run's own step-0 count by at least 2. Otherwise log CONFIRM-NEGATIVE, keep the CUSUM
     state, and continue.
     - The estimated step-0 count is 0/128 (from pre-pass majority votes; 99% upper bound 4.9%).
   - **Channel B:** a T = 0 pass on all 184 gold-unsupported training items, because C is too clean (0/128 supported at
     step 0) to show a false-accept rise. **Halt only if** a one-sided McNemar test on item flips to `supported`, against
     this run's own step-0 pass, gives p < 0.05. Otherwise log CONFIRM-NEGATIVE.
     - The estimated step-0 supported-majority share is 7–9% (Pass A 17/184, Pass B 13/184).
   - **U1 (§5.6):** a T = 0 pass on the same 184 gold-unsupported items. **Halt only if** CT on that set is above this
     run's own step-0 pass, by a one-sided McNemar test on item flips to cannot_tell, p < 0.05. Otherwise log
     CONFIRM-NEGATIVE and continue.
     - If Channel B and U1 trip in the same window, one 184-item pass serves both: it reads `supported` and
       `cannot_tell` flips.
   - **Each run measures its own T = 0 step-0 canary** (C plus the 184 items) before update 1.
   - **Cap:** at most 3 confirmations per run across Fuse A, Channel B and U1. A 4th trip of any of them halts without
     confirmation.
   - **Cost:**
     - about 18–19 GPU minutes per pass over C (75.4k completion tokens at about 65–69 tok/s, measured);
     - about 26 minutes per pass over the 184 items (estimated);
     - about 45 minutes for the step-0 pass.
     - **Worst case per run: about 2.1 h of extra GPU time** (45 min step 0 + 3 × 26 min confirmations).
   - **The false-alarm rates in §5.1–5.3 are stated WITHOUT confirmation** (the conservative figures); confirmation only
     lowers them.
6. **The other CtCollapseGuard rules,** measured over all 41 windows (none fired):
   - **S1 is DROPPED as inert** (Publisher ruling).
     - The guard reads **post-clamp** advantages. S1 needs ≥ 2 gold-supported groups where a cannot_tell sample has a
       positive advantage (`s2_grpo_smoke.py:583-603`), and the clamp forces every wrong-label advantage to ≤ 0, so S1
       can never fire.
     - The outcome S1 watched (CT rising on gold-supported items) is still watched by S2.
     - **Code assertion:** any wrong-label advantage > 0 after the clamp halts at once as a bug.
   - **S2 kept as registered:** FA ≈ 0 (1.2 × 10⁻⁵ at the upper bound; 0 in a 250k bootstrap).
   - **U1 is kept as registered (≥ 4 distinct CT items on gold-unsupported AND a correct rate < 0.78), but it no longer
     halts directly.** It goes through the T = 0 confirmation on the 184 gold-unsupported items (§5.5; Publisher ruling,
     option B, chosen because power matters more here).
     - **Its 7.1% FA20 at the Garwood upper bound is before confirmation** (2.4% at the point estimate). Confirmation
       filters it.
     - **Power against a 3× rise in CT items: 53% if the correct rate doesn't move, 93% if it falls below 0.78.**
   - The recalibration options computed for the record (not adopted). The registered rule (≥ 4 AND < 0.78) has FA 7.1%
     at the Garwood upper bound.
     - Inputs, from `v11-baselines-2-result.json` per_window_41: CT items 26 in 41 windows, λ̂ 0.634/window, Garwood
       upper 0.880; correct-rate share below the cut taken from the 41 windows. The two parts barely correlate
       (r = −0.10), so they are modelled as independent. FA20 = 1 − (1 − P(N ≥ c) · q)^20.

     | U1 option | FA20 (λ upper, q point) | FA20 (both upper) | Power vs 3× CT items (rate unchanged / rate below cut) |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | registered: ≥ 4 and < 0.78 | 7.06% | 10.2% | 52.8% / 93.2% |
     | ≥ 4 and < 0.72 | 2.41% | 5.1% | 21.9% / 93.2% |
     | ≥ 5 and < 0.78 | 1.24% | 1.8% | 23.0% / 59.6% |
     | **≥ 5 and < 0.75 (proposed A)** | **0.83%** | **1.4%** | **15.9% / 59.6%** |

     Option A (≥ 5 and < 0.75 as a direct halt) was not adopted: its power against a 3× rise is 16% when the correct
     rate doesn't move.
7. **Per-run false alarm, without confirmation:**
   - **Rules that halt directly (S2, plus the code assertions):** ≈ 0.
   - **Fuse A + Channel B + U1, before confirmation (union bound):** 2.18% + 1.91% + 7.06% = **11.2% at the upper
     bounds**; about 2.6% at the point estimates.
   - **Without U1** that union is **4.1% at the upper bounds** (Fuse A + Channel B + S2).
   - Every one of these trips goes through a T = 0 confirmation (cap 3), so the realised halt rate is lower. These
     figures are the conservative ones.

## 6. First-run canaries
Logged per window, with rollback lines anchored to the step-0 values:
- the shadow-M1 pass rate;
- accepted spans that cross a paragraph or table boundary in the raw passage;
- sign or escape slicing at span edges, and unbalanced brackets;
- wrong-label positive advantage against shadow-M1;
- quote-length p10.

The matcher returns the raw-passage span.
- **Measured** (step-0 rollouts of seeds 42–44, 120 accepted quotes, plus the 41-window spread over 14,932 quotes;
  `v11-baselines-2-result.json`):

  | Canary | Step 0 | 41-window median / max |
  |---|---|---|
  | (i) shadow-M1 pass rate | 0.857 | 0.832 / range 0.721–0.889 |
  | (ii) boundary-crossing spans | 0/120 | max 0.0078 per window |
  | (iii) sign/escape edge slices | 0/120 | 0 in all 14,932 quotes |
  | (iv) unbalanced brackets (raw span) | 0.133 | 0.113 / max 0.229 |
  | (v) wrong-label positive advantage after the clamp | — | 0 by construction |
  | (vi) quote-length p10 | 29 chars | 24 / min 18 |

- **Rollback lines.** Step 0 alone (n = 120) is too small to anchor tight lines, so each line sits beyond the extreme
  seen in the 41 windows. A line counts as crossed only for **2 consecutive windows**:
  - (i) below 0.70;
  - (ii) above 0.010;
  - (iii) ≥ 3 in one window (any single one is logged for review);
  - (iv) above 0.25;
  - (vi) below 16 chars.

  Crossing a line means stop and report (Publisher review), not an automatic halt.
- **(v) is a code assertion:** any wrong-label positive advantage after the clamp is a bug, and halts at once.
- These lines are heuristic (the extremes of 41 windows from 3 seeds). They are recorded as such, not as calibrated
  false-alarm rates.
